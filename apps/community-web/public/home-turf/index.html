California high school football teams and civfix, teaming up to keep our neighborhoods clean.

You bring the team. We bring the rest.

civfix handles the details so coaches don't have to. We supply the gear, work with city departments ahead of time, and pick a cleanup site in your high school's own neighborhood. Players don't need to sign up for anything: we send the coach the location and time, and that's it. Your job is the one you're already great at: getting a team somewhere on time and ready to work.

  • Gloves & bags
  • Tools & grabbers
  • Safety gear
  • Water
  • City coordination
  • Event logistics

No cost to your program, ever.

Why football teams?

Football teams already know how to show up. The discipline it takes to get through August two-a-days is the same discipline a great cleanup runs on: early arrivals and nobody leaving until the job is done. Home Turf puts that culture to work on the streets players grew up on, with coaches leading the way, city crews backing them up, and neighbors joining in. We believe a sixteen-year-old who takes care of their neighborhood now will still be taking care of it at forty.

Home Turf is run by Reach Out LA, the nonprofit behind civfix. You can read more about us before you get in touch.
